Lab data as the missing link for more effective chronic disease management; a value-based care approach Chronic diseases are rapidly increasing in the United States, placing a growing burden on healthcare providers, patients and payers participating in value-based care models. Managing these conditions effectively requires proactive care and a strategic approach to data-driven decision-making. These illnesses not only diminish patients' quality of life, but they also contribute to escalating healthcare costs.
